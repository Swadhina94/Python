Multiplication Operator (*)
------------------------------
1. What is the output of `5 * 4`?----->5*4=20

2. Calculate `10 * 20`.------->10*20=200

3. Find the result of `25 * 4`.------>25*4=100

4. What is the output of `2.5 * 4`?---->2.5*4=10.0

5. Calculate `-5 * 10`.--------->-5*10= -50

6. Find the result of `-5 * -10`.---->-5*-10=50

7. What is the output of `True * 10`?----->True * 10=10

8. Calculate `False * 100`.----->False * 100=0

9. Find the result of `"Hi" * 3`.----->'HiHiHi'

length=654

15. What is the output of `5 + 2 * 3`?----->5+2*3=11
